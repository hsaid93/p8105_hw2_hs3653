The dataset contains information on 1,868 subway entrances and exits,
including station names, lines, station coordinates, routes served,
entry availability, vending, entrance type, exit-only status, and ADA
compliance. I standardized column names, retained the relevant
variables, specified character types for route columns 8-11, and
converted entry availability to a logical variable. The cleaned dataset
contains 1,868 rows and 20 columns. These data are not yet tidy because
routes served are spread across 11 columns.

There are 465 distinct stations, identified by station name and line, of
which 84 are ADA compliant. Among station entrances and exits without
vending, 37.7% allow entry. After reshaping the route columns into
route-number and route-name variables, 60 distinct stations serve the A
train, of which 17 are ADA compliant.

I imported the amyloid data by skipping the first row, which contained
variable descriptions, and cleaned the column names. I treated both “NA”
and “Na” as missing values. The dataset includes 487 participants with
measurements scheduled at baseline as well as 2, 4, 6, and 8 years. I

participant ID, years since baseline, and the amyloid beta 42/40 ratio.

dataset contains 2435 rows and three columns. I retained the 172 missing
ratios to keep the scheduled time points visible even when a measurement
was unavailable.
